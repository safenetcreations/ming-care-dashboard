Ming Care Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Ming Care Customer App (com.mingcare.customer) and the Ming Care Electrician Partner App (com.mingcare.electrician) operated by Ming Care Sri Lanka (Eastern Province Operations Hub, Batticaloa).

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact & Profile Information: Name, phone number, street address, DS Division, and prominent landmark provided when requesting or fulfilling electrical repair services.
  • Camera & Photos (android.permission.CAMERA): Used strictly when a customer captures photos of an electrical fault or when a certified electrician uploads before/after repair proof and KYC trade certificates (NVQ/VTA).
  • Location Information (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used to match customers with the nearest certified electrician in Batticaloa District and assist technicians with navigation.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely to dispatch certified electricians, diagnose electrical hazards, supply genuine Ming replacement parts with warranty tracking, and issue service invoices. We do not sell personal data to third parties or display third-party advertising.

3. Data Security & Retention

All data transmitted between the mobile applications and our operations infrastructure is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Users and technicians may request profile updates or account deletion at any time by contacting our Batticaloa Operations Hub.
